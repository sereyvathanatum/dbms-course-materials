# Course guide

FESE304 Database Management System, 2026–2027: 15 weeks, with three hours of class and about six hours of self-study each week. Your instructor confirms the official dates, exam rules and assessment details.

## Learning outcomes

- **CLO1:** Explain database concepts, constraints, transactions and SQL/NoSQL choices.
- **CLO2:** Model relationships and normalize a schema to 3NF.
- **CLO3:** Write DDL, safe CRUD, filters, reports, joins, subqueries and CTEs.
- **CLO4:** Build database objects and connect an app using parameterized SQL and appropriate privileges.
- **CLO5:** Query MongoDB and Redis and justify database choices.
- **CLO6:** Protect data quality and privacy, work in a team and present results clearly.

## A typical week

In class (3 hours): team check-in and warm-up, the new topic, a break, lab tasks, a capstone sprint and a short exit ticket.

After class: finish the lab tasks and homework, write your reflection, and push your work.

## Assessment

| Component | Weight | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Attendance | 10% | |
| Weekly homework | 15% | 13 homeworks; the best 11 count |
| Midterm | 25% | Weeks 1–8 |
| Capstone project | 50% | Sprints, checkpoints, demo and individual explanation |

The capstone 50% is made up of weekly sprints (10%), Checkpoint 1 proposal (5%), Checkpoint 2 design (10%), Checkpoint 3 implementation (10%), the final project with NoSQL (10%), and individual explanation and peer evaluation (5%). Checkpoint 4 is prepared in Week 14 and delivered with the final project.

## Working with the data

- Read from `cambodia`; make all your changes in `student_work`.
- Copy results from your own output; do not type them by hand or make them up.
- Keep personal data and passwords out of your repository.
- If you use another dataset in your capstone, record its source and licence.

The lecture slides sometimes use other datasets, such as places or market prices. Those are not included here, so practise the same skills with the province, district and commune tables. Topics that this data cannot show, such as many-to-many relationships, are covered with examples on paper.
